Frequently asked questions.

Can't find what you're looking for? Use the contact form above and we'll help.

Most of our main card shows charge a small admission fee of $3–$5 to help cover venue costs. Community nights and smaller gatherings are typically free. The first 50 guests at ticketed shows also receive $10 in Apex Cash — so early arrival more than pays for the ticket. Check our Events page for specific pricing on each show.
Fill out the contact form on this page and select "Vending / Table Booking" as your inquiry type. Include what TCG products you sell and what size table you're looking for. We'll review your application within 48 hours and confirm availability. Table spots are limited and go fast — apply early. Visit our Vending page to see table tiers and pricing.
Our vendor tables typically cover Pokémon TCG, One Piece TCG, Dragon Ball Super Card Game, and Yu-Gi-Oh!. We also regularly have Lorcana, Flesh & Blood, and Magic: The Gathering product available depending on the show. Graded slabs (PSA, BGS, CGC) and sealed product are always well-represented across all games.
Absolutely. We deliberately build Apex events to be welcoming to newcomers. Our vendors are collector-focused and patient with new hobbyists. Whether you've been collecting for 20 years or you're opening your first pack at the show — Apex is for you. We especially encourage younger collectors to attend with family.
Attendee-to-attendee trading is welcome at Apex! Bring your binder, set up at a table in the community area, and make deals. However, if you plan to set up a full vendor table with product displays and regular sales, you'll need to book an official vendor spot so we can plan the floor layout appropriately.
