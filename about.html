The Heart Behind Apex

Everything we build starts with community — and every decision we make is designed to elevate the experience for every person who walks through our doors.

Why We Started

At the heart of Apex Card Show is community. We created this platform to bring people together — collectors, vendors, and fans alike — into a space that is welcoming, safe, and genuinely fun. There's nothing more rewarding than seeing people connect, smile, and enjoy the hobby we all love. That energy is what drives everything we do.

Our Vision

As collectors, vendors, and attendees ourselves, we've experienced every side of the hobby. That perspective fuels our goal to push beyond the standard and create something truly next level. We're not here to follow the norm — we're here to redefine it. Our vision is to elevate the card show experience and grow into a premier destination not just locally, but on a national stage.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: to build one of the most respected and high-quality trading card shows in the country. We strive to stand out through our attention to detail, innovation, and genuine care for the community. At Apex Card Show, we're committed to creating an experience that people remember — and a standard others aim to follow.

Our Story

From collectors to organizers.

Apex Card Show was born from a simple frustration — we couldn't find the card show we actually wanted to attend. So we decided to build it ourselves. What started as a passion project quickly became something much bigger the moment the community showed up.

Our first event in August 2024 sold out in under a week. Over 150 collectors packed the floor alongside 30+ vendors, and the energy in that room was undeniable. It confirmed everything — this community was hungry for something different, and Apex was the answer.

Today, we host multiple shows a year across Northern Virginia, partner with the DMV's most trusted vendors, and are steadily building toward something on a national scale. Every show is better than the last, and we're just getting started.
